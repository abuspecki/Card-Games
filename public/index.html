The House Rules

Manners make the game.

I.

A Fair Deal

Every hand is dealt by the house from a freshly shuffled deck. What you see on the felt is what fate intended — nobody, not even a bot, sees your cards.

II.

Chips & Coins

Members receive 5,000 chips to play with. Chips you win can be exchanged for coins, spent in the shop on card backs, felts, backgrounds and portraits. Play money only — always.

III.

Good Company

Add friends, see who is at the tables and invite them to your seat. Short of company? Anyone at a table may invite a bot.
